12V battery or hybrid battery?

A hybrid such as the Prius has two batteries. The large high-voltage battery that drives the car is a specialist job. The small 12V battery that starts the electronics is a normal car battery, and it is the one in the table above. If the car will not power up or shows warnings, the 12V battery is the first thing to check. Tell us which battery you mean when you write to us.

Can I go by my Prius's year alone?

Not safely. 2003–2026 covers several versions with different batteries. The label on your current battery, or a photo of it sent to us, settles it.
