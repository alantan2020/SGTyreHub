Forklift, Lorry and Work Vehicle Batteries in Singapore: What to Know Before You Order

A factory or a fleet is not the same job as one car. The batteries are bigger, there are more of them, and downtime costs money. Here is how work vehicle batteries differ, when to replace them, and how to get a proper quote.

Forklift, Lorry and Work Vehicle Batteries in Singapore: What to Know Before You Order

What this guide covers

Engine-powered forklifts (diesel or LPG), lorries, vans, buses and other work vehicles start on a 12V or 24V starter battery, much like a car, only larger. This guide is about those. Electric forklifts run on a large traction battery pack. That is a different product, so please ask separately instead of assuming the same answer.

How a work vehicle differs from a car

Many trucks and forklifts are 24V and use two 12V batteries connected in series. If you replace only one, the pair will be mismatched and the new one will wear out early, so both batteries should be checked together. Case sizes are often larger than car sizes, and the battery sits in a tray or box that has to match.

Constant vibration, heavy loads, dust and long idling hours shorten battery life, so the type of battery matters more than for a car.

12V or 24V: how to tell

The label on the vehicle or in the manual usually states the system voltage. A simple visual clue is two batteries side by side joined by a short cable, with the vehicle's main leads taken from the two outer terminals. That is usually a 24V system made of two 12V batteries. Do not guess. Send us a photo and we will confirm.

Why work vehicle batteries fail early

The same few causes come up again and again on forklifts and lorries:

  • Vibration loosens the battery in its tray and damages the plates inside. A hold-down that is loose or missing is a common cause.
  • Long idling and short, frequent starts mean the battery is rarely fully recharged.
  • Added equipment such as lights, radios, trackers or cameras draws power even when the engine is off.
  • Corroded or loose terminals and cables cause weak starting that looks like a bad battery.
  • A faulty alternator undercharges or overcharges the battery and wears it out quickly.
  • Mixing an old battery with a new one in a 24V pair puts extra load on the new battery.

Signs it is time to replace the batteries

Work vehicles usually give warning before they fail:

  • The engine cranks slowly, especially first thing in the morning or after the vehicle has stood overnight.
  • The vehicle needs a jump start more than once.
  • The lights dim when the starter turns.
  • The battery case is swollen, leaking or has crust around the terminals.
  • The batteries are several years old and have never been tested.

Before anyone touches the batteries

Work vehicle batteries can weigh well over 20 kg each and contain lead and acid. Switch the vehicle off, remove the key, and disconnect the negative lead first. Use proper lifting equipment and protective gloves and eyewear. If you are not sure, leave it to someone trained. The old batteries must be recycled, not left in a bin or on the road.

What to send us

The more of this you send, the faster and more accurate the quote is:

  • The type of vehicle and the make and model, for example a Toyota 8FD forklift or a 10-foot lorry.
  • The voltage, 12V or 24V, and how many batteries the vehicle has.
  • A clear photo of the label on each battery, and one photo of where the battery sits.
  • How many vehicles need batteries, and whether they can be done together.
  • Where the vehicles are, and when someone can let us in.

How we quote

For factories and fleets, the person in charge prepares the quote personally. We do not give an instant price for a job like this, because the right battery, the number of units and the location all change the answer. A call-out charge for coming to your site can apply, and we tell you the full amount before anything is arranged.

Doing several vehicles at once

If you have a few vehicles that are due, it usually makes sense to do them in one visit. Tell us the count and the models when you first write, and we can plan one trip instead of several.

Common questions

Do you replace batteries at our factory?

We can arrange a visit to your site. The person in charge confirms the details and the call-out charge with you in advance.

Can you supply batteries for electric forklifts?

Electric forklifts use a large traction battery, which is a different product. Ask us, and the person in charge will tell you whether we can help.

Is there a price for fleet orders?

Every job is quoted individually, based on the batteries needed, the number of units and the location. Send the vehicle details and we will quote.

Can I replace just one battery in a 24V forklift or lorry?

It is better to check both. If one of the pair is old and the other new, the new one carries extra load and wears out early. A tester will show whether the older one is still good.

How long do forklift and lorry batteries last?

There is no fixed number. Vibration, idling, equipment left on and the state of the charging system all change it, so test the batteries regularly instead of waiting for a failure.
