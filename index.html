Common questions

How do I find the correct tyre size for my car?

Look at the writing on the side of your tyre. The size looks like 205/55R16. The sticker inside the driver's door often shows the size the car left the factory with, but tyres may have been changed since then. Send us a photo of your tyre sidewall on WhatsApp and we'll confirm it.

Why do you ask for a photo?

Cars of the same model can use different tyre sizes depending on the year, the trim and the wheels fitted. A photo of your sidewall removes the guesswork before you buy.

How much does it cost to change tyres?

It depends on the tyre size, the brand and the fitting. Send us a photo of your tyre sidewall and we'll quote you on WhatsApp.
