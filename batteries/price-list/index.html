Battery price questions

What do these prices include?

The battery price in Singapore dollars including GST, and including fitting at one of our partner workshops. Prices were last checked on 2026-11-18 and can change, so we confirm the price with you when you send the label photo.

How do I know which code my car needs?

Look at the label on your current battery. It shows a code such as 55D23L or DIN66. Cars of the same model can use different batteries, so the label is the safest guide.

Can the price change?

Yes. Battery prices follow the supplier. The date above shows when this list was last checked.
