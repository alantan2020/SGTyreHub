Michelin Tyres in Singapore: History, Models and How to Choose

Michelin is one of the most common names on a Singapore tyre sidewall, and one of the hardest to compare, because there are many models, some of them sold in only a few sizes, and almost every claim you read comes from Michelin itself. This section of our site is our attempt to do three things in one place: tell the story of the company, explain the models you are most likely to be offered, and show you how to choose for the way you actually drive.

How we write about Michelin

We are an independent tyre retailer in Singapore. We are not an authorised Michelin dealer, and Michelin has not reviewed or approved these pages. Because we sell tyres, you should know the rules we set ourselves:

  1. We separate Michelin's claims from other people's tests. Anything taken from Michelin's own pages is marked "according to Michelin", and tests Michelin ordered are labelled as such.
  2. We report results that do not flatter the tyre. If a model came last in a test, or has a weak point such as aquaplaning, we say so.
  3. We do not invent. If we could not find a launch date or an independent test, the page says that instead of guessing.
  4. We keep prices off these pages. Prices change; message us for options in your size.

If you spot a mistake, tell us on WhatsApp and we will check it and correct the page.
