Bridgestone Tyres in Singapore: History, Ranges and How to Choose

Bridgestone is a Japanese tyre maker whose tyres are sold in Singapore. This section explains where the company came from and how its passenger-car ranges are laid out, using Bridgestone's own pages and saying plainly where we are quoting the company.

Bridgestone in Singapore: the ranges

Bridgestone Singapore groups its passenger-car tyres into families, each aimed at a different kind of driver. The descriptions below are Bridgestone's own marketing summaries from its Singapore catalogue, not our test results, and the exact models and sizes change over time, so confirm against the current catalogue before you decide.

Which range for which driver

If most of your driving is the school run, the commute and the weekend drive to the mall, the comfort range is the natural place to start. If you drive a sporty car and care about steering response, look at the performance range. If fuel use matters most, the efficiency range is aimed at that, and an SUV has its own range. A DriveGuard run-flat is only the right choice if your car was fitted with run-flats or is designed for them, so check your car's handbook.

Whatever the range, the tyre has to match your car's size, load index and speed rating. Our guide to the tyre sidewall explains how to read them.
