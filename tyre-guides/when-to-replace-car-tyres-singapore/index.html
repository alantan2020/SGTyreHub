When to Replace Your Car Tyres in Singapore

Tyres are the only part of the car that touches the road, and Singapore's heavy rain makes their condition matter more. Here is how to tell when it is time.

When to Replace Your Car Tyres in Singapore

Tread depth

The legal minimum tread depth for cars in Singapore is 1.6 mm. Most tyres have small raised bars inside the main grooves called wear indicators. When the tread is worn level with them, the tyre has reached the minimum. Safety groups often recommend replacing earlier, around 3 mm, because wet braking gets noticeably worse as the tread wears.

A quick check at home

Look at the tread at several points around the tyre and across its width. If the wear indicator bars are level with the surface in any groove, it is time. Feel for uneven wear across the tread, which can point to a pressure or alignment problem.

Damage that means replace now

A bulge or bubble on the sidewall, a cut that reaches the cords, a nail or screw in the sidewall, and deep cracks in the rubber all call for a new tyre. A repair is not suitable for these. Do not keep driving on a tyre with a bulge.

Age

Tyres harden with age even when they look fine. See our guide on the DOT code to find how old yours are. Tyres that are many years old deserve an inspection.

Signs while driving

Longer stopping distances in the rain, a car that aquaplanes on wet roads, a steady vibration that was not there before, and a car that pulls to one side are all reasons to have the tyres looked at.

Common questions

Do I need to replace all four tyres at once?

Not always. On a two-wheel-drive car, replacing a pair on the same axle is common. On an all-wheel-drive car, the tyres should be of similar wear, so ask before replacing only some.

Can a tyre with a puncture be repaired?

A small puncture in the central tread can often be repaired properly by a workshop. Sidewall damage cannot.

How often should I check my tyres?

A look at the tread and a pressure check once a month is a good habit, and always before a long trip.
