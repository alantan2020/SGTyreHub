Honda Civic tyre sizes in Singapore

The Honda Civic can take more than one tyre size, depending on the year, the trim and the wheels fitted. Send us a photo of your tyre sidewall and we'll confirm yours and what's in stock.

Kah Motor (Honda Singapore) lists 215/55R16 (1.5L Turbo DOHC VTEC) and 215/50R17 (2.0L DOHC i-VTEC e:HEV) for the current Honda Civic, 11th generation. Your car may differ, so please confirm yours with a photo.
