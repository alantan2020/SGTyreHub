What to know about the Honda CR-V battery

Many Honda CR-V cars (2012–2024) use a battery in the 85D23L size range. The exact battery depends on your car's year, engine and equipment, so please check the label on your own battery.

Some CR-V cars have automatic engine stop-start. If yours does, or if the label says EFB or AGM, the new battery must be the same type or better. Tell us and we will match it.
