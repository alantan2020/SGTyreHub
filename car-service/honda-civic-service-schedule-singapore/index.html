Honda Civic Service Schedule in Singapore: Packages A to E Explained

Honda Singapore groups routine servicing into packages A to E. Which one your Civic needs depends on its mileage if it is a non-turbo car, or on how many services it has had if it is a turbo car. Here is how the packages line up and what each one covers, based on Honda Singapore's published service packages.

Honda Civic Service Schedule in Singapore: Packages A to E Explained

How Honda Singapore sets the service interval

Honda Singapore's service packages use full synthetic engine oil, changed every 10,000 km or 6 months. Honda also states that for new Honda vehicles, periodic maintenance has to be carried out according to the recommended maintenance schedule under the warranty terms, so check your warranty booklet if your car is still covered.

The packages are called A, B, C, D and E. Package A and package E are the lighter ones, package B adds more inspections, package C is the one with the larger replacements, and package D is the spark plug service.

Non-turbo Civic: packages by mileage

Honda lists the Civic 1.6A, 1.8A and 2.0A as non-turbo cars. For these, the package follows the odometer reading:

  • Package A: 10,000 km, 30,000 km, 50,000 km, 70,000 km and 90,000 km
  • Package B: 20,000 km, 60,000 km, 140,000 km and 180,000 km
  • Package C: 40,000 km, 80,000 km, 120,000 km and 160,000 km
  • Package D: 100,000 km
  • Package E: 110,000 km, 130,000 km, 150,000 km, 170,000 km and 190,000 km

Turbo Civic: packages by service number

Honda lists the Civic Turbo 1.5A and the Civic Turbo Hatchback 1.5A as turbo cars. For these, the package follows the number of services the car has had, not the odometer:

  • Package A: 2nd, 4th, 6th, 8th and 10th service
  • Package B: 3rd, 7th, 15th and 19th service
  • Package C: 5th, 9th, 13th and 17th service
  • Package D: 11th service
  • Package E: 12th, 14th, 16th, 18th and 20th service

What each package covers

Every package replaces the engine oil and the oil filter, and every package includes a set of inspections, so the main differences are in what else is replaced or checked more closely.

  • Package A: engine oil and oil filter, air cleaner element cleaned or replaced if necessary, and a general inspection that includes the brakes, shock absorbers, steering, battery and its terminals, lights, wipers and the tyre sidewalls and tread wear.
  • Package B: the same as A, with more inspections added, including the exhaust, fuel lines, fuel injectors and the drive belts where fitted. A 4x4 differential oil change is listed for cars that have one.
  • Package C: the larger service. It replaces the transmission oil, the brake fluid and the fuel filter or strainer on top of the oil and oil filter, and it carries the wider set of inspections from package B.
  • Package D: the 100,000 km service, which replaces the spark plugs together with the oil and oil filter.
  • Package E: oil and oil filter with the routine inspections, for the later mileages.

Is my Civic turbo or non-turbo?

Check the engine size in your car's registration details or on the badge. A 1.5-litre Civic sold in Singapore is a turbo, while the 1.6, 1.8 and 2.0-litre Civics are non-turbo cars on Honda's list. If you are not sure, ask your service centre which list your car is on before you decide when it is due.

These are Honda Singapore's published packages. What your own car needs can differ, and your car's service prompts and your authorised service centre have the final say.

Use the service as a chance to look at tyres and the battery

Common questions

How often does a Honda Civic need servicing in Singapore?

Honda Singapore's service packages use engine oil changed every 10,000 km or 6 months, so most Civics are due at least twice a year if they are not driven much.

Is a Civic 1.6 a turbo?

No. Honda Singapore lists the Civic 1.6A, 1.8A and 2.0A as non-turbo cars. The turbo Civics are the 1.5-litre models.

What does Honda service package C include?

Package C is due at 40,000 km, 80,000 km, 120,000 km and 160,000 km for non-turbo cars, and at the 5th, 9th, 13th and 17th service for turbo cars. It replaces the transmission oil, brake fluid and fuel filter along with the engine oil and oil filter.

When are the spark plugs replaced?

In package D. Honda lists it at 100,000 km for non-turbo cars and at the 11th service for turbo cars.

Do I have to follow the package schedule?

Honda states that for new Honda vehicles, periodic maintenance has to be done according to the recommended schedule under the warranty terms. Read your warranty booklet or ask your Honda service centre about your own car.
