Does your car battery need replacing?

Most car batteries in Singapore last a few years, and the heat, short trips and long gaps between drives all shorten that. These are the signs worth acting on, each with a guide that explains what to check first:

  • The engine cranks slowly or the car will not start: is it the battery or something else?
  • The battery is flat again after a night or a few days: causes of a battery draining overnight.
  • The battery warning light is on, or the battery goes flat after long drives: battery, alternator or belt?
  • White or blue-green crust on the terminals: how to clean it and when it means a new battery.
  • The battery is several years old: how long a car battery lasts in Singapore.

Standard, EFB or AGM: which type does your car need?

The replacement has to match the type your car was built for. A car with automatic engine stop-start needs an EFB or AGM battery, and many European cars also need the new battery registered with the car's computer. The label on your current battery shows the size code and often the type. Our guides explain EFB, AGM and standard batteries, what the size codes mean and why European cars need registration.

Car battery questions

How do I know which battery my car needs?

Look at the label on your current battery. It shows a code such as 55D23L, 95D26L or DIN66, and sometimes the words EFB or AGM. Take a clear photo of the label and send it to us on WhatsApp, together with your car model.

Why not just go by my car model?

Cars of the same model can use different batteries depending on the year, the engine and the equipment. The label on your own battery removes the guesswork.

Does my car need an EFB or AGM battery?

If your car has automatic engine stop-start, or the label on your battery says EFB or AGM, the new battery must be the same type or better. A standard battery in a stop-start car wears out early and can switch the stop-start function off.

What about European cars and electric cars?

Many European cars need the new battery registered with the car's computer, and electric cars use a separate 12V battery. Send us your car model, year and a photo of the label and we will confirm the details with you.
