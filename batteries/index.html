Car battery questions

How do I know which battery my car needs?

Look at the label on your current battery. It shows a code such as 55D23L, 95D26L or DIN66, and sometimes the words EFB or AGM. Take a clear photo of the label and send it to us on WhatsApp, together with your car model.

Why not just go by my car model?

Cars of the same model can use different batteries depending on the year, the engine and the equipment. The label on your own battery removes the guesswork.

Does my car need an EFB or AGM battery?

If your car has automatic engine stop-start, or the label on your battery says EFB or AGM, the new battery must be the same type or better. A standard battery in a stop-start car wears out early and can switch the stop-start function off.

What about European cars and electric cars?

Many European cars need the new battery registered with the car's computer, and electric cars use a separate 12V battery. Send us your car model, year and a photo of the label and we will confirm the details with you.

What does it cost?

It depends on the battery. Send us a photo of the label on WhatsApp and we will quote you.
