How to Read Your Tyre Sidewall: 205/55R16 91V Explained

The writing on the side of your tyre is a complete description of its size and what it can carry. Once you know how to read it, ordering the right tyre is easy.

How to Read Your Tyre Sidewall: 205/55R16 91V Explained

The size: 205/55R16

Take a typical size such as 205/55R16. The first number, 205, is the width of the tyre in millimetres. The second number, 55, is the height of the sidewall as a percentage of the width, called the aspect ratio. Here the sidewall is 55 percent of 205 mm tall. The letter R means radial construction, which is what virtually all car tyres use. The last number, 16, is the diameter of the wheel in inches that the tyre fits.

The load index and speed rating: 91V

After the size you will usually see a number and a letter, such as 91V. The number is the load index, a code for the weight each tyre can carry. The letter is the speed rating, the highest speed the tyre is designed for when properly inflated and in good condition. A V rating is up to 240 km/h and an H rating is up to 210 km/h. Replacement tyres should have a load index and speed rating at least equal to what your car was fitted with.

Other markings you may notice

XL or Reinforced means the tyre is built for a higher load. M+S marks a tyre meant for mud and snow. A small arrow with the word Rotation or Outside tells you which way the tyre must be fitted. The DOT code, a series of letters and digits starting with DOT, includes the week and year the tyre was made. Our guide on tyre age explains how to read it.

Why the sidewall beats the car model

The same car model can come with different wheel sizes depending on the year and the trim level. The tyre size printed on your tyres is the most reliable starting point. If you have changed your wheels, the size may be different from what the car left the factory with.

Common questions

What does the R in 205/55R16 mean?

R stands for radial, the construction used by almost all modern car tyres. The number after it is the wheel diameter in inches.

Can I fit a different size from the one on my tyres?

A small change is sometimes possible, but the overall diameter, the load index and the clearance in the wheel arch all matter. Ask us before you decide, and keep to your owner's manual where you can.

What if I cannot read the size?

Wipe the sidewall clean and photograph it at an angle with side light, which makes the raised letters easier to see. Or tell us your car model and year and we will help you narrow it down.
