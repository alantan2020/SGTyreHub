Batteries for Forklifts, Lorries and Factory Vehicles in Singapore

A factory or a fleet is not the same job as one car. The batteries are bigger, there are more of them, and downtime costs money. Here is how to get a proper quote.

Batteries for Forklifts, Lorries and Factory Vehicles in Singapore

What this guide covers

Engine-powered forklifts, lorries, vans, buses and other work vehicles start on a 12V or 24V starter battery, much like a car, only larger. This guide is about those. Electric forklifts run on a large traction battery pack. That is a different product, so please ask us about it separately instead of assuming the same answer.

How a work vehicle differs from a car

Many trucks and forklifts are 24V and use two 12V batteries connected in series. If you replace only one, the pair will be mismatched and the new one will wear out early, so we look at both. Case sizes are often larger than car sizes, and the battery sits in a tray or box that has to match. Constant vibration and long idling hours shorten battery life, so the type of battery matters more than for a car.

What to send us

The more of this you send, the faster and more accurate the quote is:

  • The type of vehicle and the make and model, for example a Toyota 8FD forklift or a 10-foot lorry.
  • The voltage, 12V or 24V, and how many batteries the vehicle has.
  • A clear photo of the label on each battery, and one photo of where the battery sits.
  • How many vehicles need batteries, and whether they can be done together.
  • Where the vehicles are, and when someone can let us in.

How we quote

For factories and fleets, the person in charge prepares the quote personally. We do not give an instant price for a job like this, because the right battery, the number of units and the location all change the answer. A call-out charge for coming to your site can apply, and we tell you the full amount before anything is arranged.

Doing several vehicles at once

If you have a few vehicles that are due, it usually makes sense to do them in one visit. Tell us the count and the models when you first write, and we can plan one trip instead of several.

Common questions

Do you replace batteries at our factory?

We can arrange a visit to your site. The person in charge confirms the details and the call-out charge with you in advance.

Can you supply batteries for electric forklifts?

Electric forklifts use a large traction battery, which is a different product. Ask us, and the person in charge will tell you whether we can help.

Is there a price for fleet orders?

Every job is quoted individually, based on the batteries needed, the number of units and the location. Send the vehicle details and we will quote.
