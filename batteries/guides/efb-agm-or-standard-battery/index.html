EFB, AGM or Standard Battery? Why Stop-Start Cars Cannot Go Cheaper

If your car shuts its engine off at traffic lights, the battery is doing far more work than in an ordinary car. That is why the type matters.

EFB, AGM or Standard Battery? Why Stop-Start Cars Cannot Go Cheaper

The three types in plain words

A standard (flooded or maintenance-free) battery is built for starting the engine and running the electrics of an ordinary car. An EFB (enhanced flooded battery) is a stronger flooded design for cars with basic stop-start. An AGM (absorbent glass mat) battery is sealed, handles repeated deep discharge and fast recharging better, and is used in many cars with stop-start, regenerative braking and heavy electrical loads.

Why stop-start cars need EFB or AGM

A stop-start car restarts the engine many times in a single trip, and the battery also powers the air-conditioning, lights and screens while the engine is off. A plain standard battery wears out much faster under this load. It can also cause the stop-start system to stop working, because the car's computer will not stop the engine when it thinks the battery is weak.

The rule: same type or better, never lower

If the label on your old battery says EFB, the replacement can be EFB or AGM. If it says AGM, the replacement must be AGM. A standard battery is never an acceptable replacement for either. A cheaper battery of the wrong type may work for a few weeks and then fail early, so it is not a saving.

How to tell what your car needs

The most reliable way is the label on your current battery. It usually says EFB or AGM if it is one of those types. Also look at the car itself:

  • Does the engine switch off at traffic lights and restart when you lift off the brake? Then the car has stop-start.
  • Is there an Auto Stop, i-stop, Idle Stop or Start-Stop button or light on the dashboard?
  • Is the battery in the boot or under a seat, instead of under the bonnet? Many European cars do this and use AGM.

Common questions

Can I replace an AGM battery with an EFB battery?

No. An AGM battery should be replaced with an AGM battery. An EFB battery can be replaced with EFB or AGM.

My car has no stop-start. Do I still need EFB or AGM?

Not unless the label on your old battery says so. A car without stop-start and with a standard battery can use a standard replacement of the correct size.

Will a standard battery damage my stop-start car?

It is unlikely to cause lasting damage, but it will wear out faster, and the stop-start system may be switched off by the car. The type on the old label is the guide.
