Check your own car

  • Read your log cardYour vehicle registration details show the model, year and chassis number.
  • Protect the chassis numberA parts seller may ask for it to confirm a part. Give it only to a seller you have chosen, and never post it in public.
  • Look at the actual carIt may have been repaired or modified before. Compare the part on your car, not only the model name.
