About SG Tyre Hub

We help car owners in Singapore find the right tyre size for their car, then supply the tyres.

Send your sidewall photo on WhatsApp

Who we are

SG Tyre Hub is operated by SG Lube Company Pte Ltd, Singapore. We supply car tyres and arrange fitting through partner tyre workshops.

How it works

  1. Take a clear photo of the writing on the side of your tyre. The size looks like 205/55R16.
  2. Send it to us on WhatsApp. We confirm the exact size for your car and check what is in stock.
  3. We send you a quote on WhatsApp. If you go ahead, we arrange the fitting.

Why we ask for a photo

Cars of the same model can use different tyre sizes depending on the year, the trim and the wheels fitted, and tyres may have been changed since the car was new. A photo of your sidewall removes the guesswork before you buy.

Where the sizes on this website come from

Tyre sizes on our model pages are candidate sizes from the public vehicle database cardata.wiki (CC BY 4.0). Where we show sizes published by a car brand's Singapore distributor, the page links to the source and says when we checked it. These are guides only. The size printed on your own tyre is the one that counts.

Prices

We do not show prices on this website. We quote on WhatsApp once your tyre size is confirmed.
