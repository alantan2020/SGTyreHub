Which Civic do you have?

Check your own car

  • Look at your vehicle's registration details (log card) for the model, year and chassis number.
  • A parts seller may ask for the chassis number to confirm the exact part. Give it only to a seller you have chosen, and never post it in public.
  • Cars may have been modified or repaired before. Compare the part on your car, not only the model name.
