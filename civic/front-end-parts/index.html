Front end part names for Civic owners

Before you ask a workshop or a parts seller, find the number on the photo and use the name next to it.

Names in English and Chinese

  1. 1
    Bonnet (hood)引擎盖The large panel that opens over the engine.
  2. 2
    Front grille中网The vented section between the headlights.
  3. 3
    Grille upper trim (moulding)中网上饰条The bar or trim strip across the top part of the grille.
  4. 4
    Front emblem (badge)车标The badge on the nose of the car.
  5. 5
    Front bumper cover前保险杠The large painted plastic shell across the front.
  6. 6
    Headlight assembly大灯总成The whole lamp unit, including its mounting tabs.
  7. 7
    Front fender前翼子板（叶子板）The body panel above each front wheel.
  8. 8
    Bumper side bracket保险杠侧支架A hidden holder that keeps the bumper edge tight against the fender.
  9. 9
    Retaining clips卡扣Small fasteners that hold plastic parts in place.
  10. 10
    Fog light and surround雾灯和雾灯框The lower lamp, if fitted, and the trim around it.
  11. 11
    Lower grille下格栅The air opening low in the bumper.
  12. 12
    Front lip (lower spoiler)前唇（下护板）The strip along the bottom edge of the bumper.

Say which side, and ask the right question

“Left” and “right” normally mean as seen from the driver's seat, facing forward. On a right-hand-drive car in Singapore, the left side is the front passenger side. Parts listings may write LH and RH. Add a photo so nobody has to guess.

Do not assume a small part is sold on its own, or that you must replace a whole assembly. Ask the seller: “Is this part sold separately for my car, and what comes with it?”
